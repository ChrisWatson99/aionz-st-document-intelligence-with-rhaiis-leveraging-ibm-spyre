Closing summary

The repository delivers a working upload, summarize, store and chat application that follows the documented architecture. The main shortfalls are the unsupported Spyre-acceleration claims, mismatches between the documented RHAIIS command and the client code, a broken multilingual summary path, a health check that cannot fail, and the absence of authentication. Resolving the P1 items makes the demo reliable and safe to show; P2 aligns the product with its documentation; P3 prepares it for repeatable deployment.

This review is static. The application was not built or run, so behaviour on Spyre hardware was not verified.
